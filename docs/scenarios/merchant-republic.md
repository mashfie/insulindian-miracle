# Merchant Republic

## Mechanism

This is the most aggressively trade-mediated scenario in the suite.

Key overrides:

- very high initial openness,
- strong network population, capital, and density gains,
- lower curse pressure,
- explicit trade-cluster bonuses for accessibility, openness, and capital.

## Literature analogue

- [Krugman (1991)](https://www.nber.org/papers/w3275)
- [Christaller (1933/1966)](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL20675263M/Central_places_in_Southern_Germany)

The intended reading is not "Venice in code" but "port-led hierarchy and exchange advantages as a stylized regime."

## Expected signatures

- high value of openness-sensitive policies,
- stronger payoff to clustered port sites,
- less sensitivity to raw mineral rents than in [[resource-curse-scenario]].
