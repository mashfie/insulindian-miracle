# Megacity Trap

## Mechanism

This scenario rewards concentration early and punishes it later.

Key overrides:

- weak secondary-city bonus,
- high metropolis penalty,
- lower overstretch threshold,
- somewhat stronger curse pressure.

## Literature analogue

- [Henderson (1974)](https://ideas.repec.org/a/aea/aecrev/v64y1974i4p640-56.html)
- [Gabaix (1999)](https://academic.oup.com/qje/article/114/3/739/1848099)

The mechanism is a stylized city-size diseconomy regime, not an estimated megacity model.

## Expected signatures

- steeper concentration,
- more negative Zipf slope,
- sharper divergence between early and late rewards for dominant sites.
