# Botswana

## Mechanism

This is the high-resource, high-institutional-quality counterpoint to [[resource-curse-scenario]].

Key overrides:

- lower `resource_curse_strength`,
- much stronger openness and capital buffers,
- higher inclusive investment and productivity gains,
- very low `initial_extraction_resource_bias`,
- higher initial openness and adaptability shape parameters.

## Literature analogue

The note is best read through the conditional-cure argument:

- [Mehlum, Moene, and Torvik (2006)](https://academic.oup.com/ej/article/116/508/1/5089390)
- [Acemoglu, Johnson, and Robinson (2005)](https://www.nber.org/papers/w10481)

The scenario name signals a resource-rich institutional exception, not a literal Botswana calibration.

## Expected signatures

- lower terminal extraction than in [[resource-curse-scenario]],
- stronger persistence of openness and capital,
- smaller performance penalty for simple policies because the environment self-stabilizes more often.
