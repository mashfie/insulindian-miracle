# Shock Reform

## Mechanism

This scenario activates the full post-shock transition and legacy machinery.

Key overrides:

- positive `shock_probability`,
- resource-biased shock targeting,
- stronger reform step,
- nonzero transition duration, extraction cap, and capital/openness gains,
- nonzero legacy fade and readiness weighting.

## Literature analogue

- [Hoj et al. (2006)](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/the-political-economy-of-structural-reform_881353527404.html)
- [Li et al. (2021)](https://www.nber.org/papers/w28994)
- [Gallego, Maldonado, and Trujillo (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jebo.2020.07.006)

The code treats crisis as a reduced-form window that can lower extraction and rebuild capital. It is not a legislative or coalition model of reform.

## Expected signatures

- more reform events,
- stronger gap between high-readiness and low-readiness sites,
- potential reversal of early extractive advantage after shocks.
